Topic A: Flat Shapes

Perimeter, area, circles, and right triangles.

01 — Perimeter & Area

Perimeter & Area

Perimeter is the distance around a shape. Area is the space inside it.

  • Rectangle: P = 2l + 2w, A = l × w.
  • Triangle: A = ½ × b × h.
  • Parallelogram: A = b × h. Trapezoid: A = ½ × h × (b₁ + b₂).
  • Units: perimeter is in units (ft), area is in square units (ft²).
Example: A room is 12 ft by 10 ft. Area = 120 ft². Perimeter = 44 ft.

02 — Circles & Right Triangles

Circles & the Pythagorean Theorem

Circles use π (about 3.14). Right triangles use a² + b² = c².

  • Circumference: C = 2πr or C = πd.
  • Area of a circle: A = πr².
  • Radius vs diameter: the diameter is twice the radius.
  • Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c², where c is the long side across from the right angle.
Example: Legs 6 and 8: 36 + 64 = 100, so c = √100 = 10.

Topic B: Solid Shapes

Volume and surface area of 3D figures.

03 — Volume

Volume of Solids

Volume is how much a 3D shape can hold. It's measured in cubic units (ft³).

  • Rectangular prism (box): V = l × w × h.
  • Cylinder: V = πr²h.
  • Cone: V = ⅓πr²h. Pyramid: V = ⅓ × base area × h.
  • Sphere: V = ⁴⁄₃πr³.
Example: A box 4 × 3 × 2 holds 24 cubic units.

04 — Surface Area

Surface Area

Surface area is the total area of all the outside faces, like the wrapping paper for a gift.

  • Rectangular prism: SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh.
  • Cube: SA = 6s².
  • Cylinder: SA = 2πrh + 2πr².
  • Tip: volume = filling it up; surface area = covering it.
Example: A cube with sides 3: SA = 6 × 9 = 54 square units.
